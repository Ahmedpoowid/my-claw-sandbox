# Narration script: "How to Make a Presentation in PowerPoint"

The video `PowerPoint-Tutorial.mp4` is 2:08 long and has captions on screen, but no voice.
Read the lines below while it plays. Each block starts at the time shown, so you can follow along.
Speak at a relaxed pace, about the speed you'd use to explain something to a friend.

| Time | On screen | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title | Let's make a PowerPoint presentation in seven easy steps, from a blank slide to a finished video. |
| 0:07 | Step 1 · Start a new presentation | Step one. Open PowerPoint, go to **File**, then **New**, and click **Blank Presentation**. Your first slide is a title slide. Click the box that says "Click to add title" and type your title, then add a subtitle underneath. |
| 0:22 | Step 2 · Pick a theme | Step two: pick a theme. Open the **Design** tab and choose a theme from the gallery. Then use **Variants** to try different colours. Every slide updates at once, so your whole deck stays consistent. |
| 0:35 | Step 3 · Add slides | Step three: add slides. On the **Home** tab, click the arrow under **New Slide** and pick a layout. **Title and Content** works for most slides. |
| 0:47 | Step 4 · Add your text | Step four: add your text. Click into the title box and type, then click the body box and type your points, pressing **Enter** for each new bullet. Keep it short: three to five bullets, about six words each. |
| 1:01 | Step 5 · Add visuals | Step five: add visuals. Go to **Insert**, then **Icons**, search, pick one and click **Insert**. For numbers, use **Insert**, then **Chart**, and type your data into the sheet that opens. One strong visual per slide is enough. |
| 1:15 | Step 6 · Transitions & animations | Step six: transitions and animations. On the **Transitions** tab, choose **Fade**, and click **Apply To All** so every slide matches. Then select your text, open **Animations** and choose **Fade**, so your bullets appear one at a time while you talk. |
| 1:30 | Step 7 · Present it | Step seven: present. Practise first with **Rehearse with Coach** on the **Slide Show** tab. It gives you feedback on your pacing. When you're ready, click **From Beginning**, or press **F5**. Click to move forward, and press **Escape** to finish. |
| 1:44 | Bonus · Save it as a video | Bonus: turn it into a video. Go to **File**, **Export**, **Create a Video**. Choose **Full HD**, click **Create Video** and save it as an MP4. Want your voice in it? Record your narration first with the **Record** tab. |
| 1:59 | Recap | That's it: theme, slides, text, visuals, motion and practice. Now open PowerPoint and make yours. |

## Adding your voice to the video

You have two simple options:

1. **Play it live.** Play the MP4 during your presentation and read the script out loud, like a live commentary.
2. **Record a voiceover.** Open the MP4 in any video editor that can record a voiceover, for example Clipchamp on Windows, iMovie on a Mac or CapCut on your phone. Record yourself reading the script, then export the new video.

## Tips for recording

- Read the whole script out loud once before recording.
- Record somewhere quiet, with the microphone about a hand's width from your mouth.
- If you trip over a line, pause for a second and say it again. You can cut the mistake out afterwards.
