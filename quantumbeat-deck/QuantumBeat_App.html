How QuantumBeat works

01 · INPUTThe heart produces a tiny magnetic field with each beat.
02 · QUANTUM SENSORLaser-prepared atom spins respond to the magnetic field.
03 · PROCESSSoftware filters noise and detects individual beats.
04 · STATISTICSBeat gaps are compared with a healthy reference baseline.
05 · OUTPUTUnusual gaps are flagged for a doctor to review.

Project features

  • Contactless magnetic sensing concept.
  • Noise filtering before beat detection.
  • Z-score screening to flag unusual beat intervals.
  • Designed to support clinical review, not replace doctors.
