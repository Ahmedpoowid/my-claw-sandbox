THE PROBLEM
Heart rhythm problems are easy to miss
17.9 million
people died from cardiovascular diseases in 2019, about 32% of all deaths worldwide.
Source: World Health Organization, cardiovascular diseases fact sheet

Rhythm problems come and go

A short check-up can miss them, so they are found late.

ECG needs electrodes

Every test needs sticky electrodes on the skin.

SQUID scanners are rare

Very sensitive, but they need extreme cooling and shielded rooms.

Finding rhythm problems early gives doctors more time to treat them.
THE QUANTUM SCIENCE
Quantum sensors read the heart's magnetic field
1 · PREPARELaser light lines up the atom spins.
2 · EXPOSEEach heartbeat's tiny field tilts the spins.
3 · READ OUTThe light shows how much they tilted.
4 · COMPARESoftware checks every gap between beats.
Optically pumped magnetometers work at room temperature, with no extreme cooling.  Research basis: Morales et al., 2017 · SandboxAQ, 2024 · Feng et al., 2025

WHAT MAKES IT SPECIAL
Three key features
01

Contactless sensing

HOW IT WORKS

Quantum sensors read the heart's magnetic field from outside the body.

WHY IT MATTERS

No electrodes or skin contact, so it is more comfortable.

02

Smart noise filtering

HOW IT WORKS

Filters remove drift and mains noise before the beats are found.

WHY IT MATTERS

Heart signals are faint, so clean data means fewer false alarms.

03

Statistical early alert

HOW IT WORKS

Each beat gap is compared with a healthy baseline using z-scores.

WHY IT MATTERS

Unusual beats are flagged for a doctor to check.

Unlike cooled SQUID scanners, optically pumped sensors work at  room temperature.
