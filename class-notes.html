Class Recorder Notes

Turn a recorded lesson into a summary, flashcards and a quiz.

1 · Record the class
  1. Ask your teacher first. Recording people without permission can break school rules and UAE law.
  2. Record with your iPad or phone's recorder app. Put it near the teacher.
  3. After class, open the recording and copy its transcript (text). Many recorder apps can make one.
  4. Paste it below. Add photos of the board or slides if you have them.
